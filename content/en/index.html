FAQ

Do you only work with SAP?

No. My background is mainly SAP, but I advise independently of vendors – including Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle and ERP systems for mid-sized companies. What matters is what fits your business.

What does the ECC end of maintenance in 2027 mean for us?

Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC 6.0 ends at the end of 2027. After that there are paid extension options until 2030 and, under conditions, beyond. Which option makes economic sense depends on your landscape, timeline and target picture – more here.

Do you also build solutions that are not in the standard?

Yes, that is a core focus. I develop extensions, interfaces, portals and services beside the ERP core – tested, documented and built so that updates stay manageable.

How do you use AI?

Where it measurably takes work away – for example with documents, variance analysis or preparing purchasing negotiations. Decisions with impact, such as postings or payments, are always confirmed by a person.

How does working together start?

With a free first conversation. Then I propose a compact start – usually a workshop with a clear result – and you decide how to continue.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. I have supported programmes across several countries and work in German, English and French, on site or remote.
