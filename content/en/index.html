FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who will I work with?

With Marcel Wagner personally – from the first call to go-live. STEGARC is a focused advisory practice, not a large firm with changing teams.

Do you only work with SAP?

No. The background is mainly SAP, but STEGARC advises independently of vendors – including Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle and ERP systems for mid-sized companies.

What does the ECC end of maintenance in 2027 mean for us?

Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC 6.0 ends at the end of 2027. Paid extensions run until 2030 and, under conditions, beyond. More on the blog.

Is the STEGARC platform ready for production?

Not yet. It is a working prototype with a public live demo on synthetic data. Advisory and delivery services do not depend on it.

How do you use AI?

Where it measurably takes work away. Decisions with impact such as postings or payments are always confirmed by a person.

Are there references?

Yes. Clients are deliberately not named on the website; references and contacts are shared in a personal conversation.

How does working together start?

With a free first call. Then a compact start is proposed, usually a workshop with a clear result.
