Privacy policy

This is a courtesy translation. The German Datenschutzerklärung is legally binding.

With this policy I inform you pursuant to Art. 13 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) which personal data is processed when you visit this website. It applies to this homepage (marcelwagner-afk.github.io/stegarc-homepage) and to the STEGARC product page, live demo and prototype (marcelwagner-afk.github.io/stegarc-suite).

1. Controller

Marcel Wagner – STEGARC (sole proprietorship)
Eichendorffstr. 38, 75031 Eppingen, Germany
E-mail: marcel.wagner@w-dfs.de

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so.

2. In brief

  • No cookies are set. The homepage stores nothing in your browser; the live demo only remembers your settings locally (see section 5).
  • There is no tracking, no web analytics, no advertising and no social media plugins.
  • All files including fonts, films and program libraries are delivered by the hosting provider; no content is loaded from third parties.
  • I only process personal data if you contact me.

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When pages are accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, date and time of access, the requested file, the referrer and information about browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its security and stability. I have no access to these log data myself.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing this website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). Details in GitHub’s privacy statement.

4. Contact by e-mail or phone

If you contact me by e-mail or phone, I process your details (e.g. name, contact data, content of the enquiry) to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, otherwise my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

I delete your data once the enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Business letters and tax-relevant documents are kept in accordance with commercial and tax law (generally six or ten years, § 257 HGB, § 147 AO).

E-mails are transmitted and stored via my e-mail provider, which processes the data on my behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).

5. Product page, live demo and prototype

The product page, live demo and prototype are also provided via GitHub Pages; section 3 applies. The demo uses only synthetic sample data of fictitious companies. Entries in the demo are not transmitted to me or to third parties.

So that the demo keeps your settings (selected language, demo progress, view), it stores them in your browser’s local storage. The data does not leave your device and is not read by me. This storage is strictly necessary for the function you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); further processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can delete the data at any time in your browser settings.

The product page refers to sources of other providers. Data is only transferred to them when you click such a link; the privacy notices of the respective provider then apply.

6. Your rights

You have the right at any time to

  • access the data processed about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

Simply contact me at the e-mail address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.

7. Obligation to provide data, automated decisions

You are not obliged to provide personal data; without it, however, I cannot answer an enquiry. No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.
